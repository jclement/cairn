FAQ

Frequently anticipated questions.

The questions are absurd. The answers are mostly true.

Will Cairn change my life?

Statistically, yes. Practically, it gives your AI assistant a place to keep markdown notes between conversations, and a way to find them again. Whether that changes your life depends on how much you were repeating yourself before.

What actually is it?

A memory server for AI assistants, running as a Cloudflare Worker. Your agent connects over MCP (with OAuth), and gets tools to write, read, edit, search, list and delete notes, and to follow the links between them. Notes use the same markdown format as Basic Memory: observations like - [preference] … and relations like - relates_to [[Other note]]. You sign in with a passkey, and there’s a web viewer for browsing what your agents have saved.

Where do my memories go when I close the tab? Do they get cold?

They stay warm in a SQLite database inside a Cloudflare Durable Object, one per vault. Search indexes (full-text and vectors) are built from your notes and can always be rebuilt from them. The markdown is the source of truth; nothing exists only in an index.

Is my data used to train an AI that will one day replace me?

Cairn doesn’t train anything, and there’s no language model on the server. To make semantic search work, note text is turned into embeddings with an open embedding model on Cloudflare Workers AI. Your clever agent does the thinking; the server just stores and finds.

What if I forget my passkey? Isn’t that a bit ironic?

Deeply. At signup you get ten single-use recovery codes: save them somewhere that isn’t Cairn. Adding a second passkey (say, your phone and your laptop) is the best insurance. There is no email reset, because there is no email.

Can I leave? Will you be sad?

Yes, and only a little. Settings → Download everything gives you a zip of every note as markdown, with [[links]] intact, ready for Obsidian or Basic Memory.

Which agents work with it?

Anything that speaks remote MCP with OAuth. claude.ai and Claude Code are the tested path; the connect guide walks through both in about two minutes.

What happens if there are snakes on my plane?

Cairn will remember which plane. Beyond that, frankly, you’re on your own.
